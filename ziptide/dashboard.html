Method · Weight sensitivity analysis

Do the conclusions depend on our arbitrary weights?

Re-scoring under 4 weight schemes to test robustness.
Method: gut-support recomputed as a weighted sum of the same binary/ordinal features under alternate weight vectors. If the star and bottom recipes stay stable, our narrative does not hinge on the exact weights — the key limitation reviewers will probe. Companion methods (not shown): cross-tabulation, and inter-rater reliability (Krippendorff's α) once students double-code.
